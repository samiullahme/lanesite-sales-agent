---
name: calibrate
description: Turn the owner's feedback on a bad reply into a general rule. Only the owner runs this; never run it on your own.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

1. Read the conversation and the owner's feedback.
2. Explain in 1–2 lines WHY the reply was wrong.
3. Propose ONE general rule (not a fix for just this lead), e.g. "Clinics ko 'customer' nahi, 'patient' kaho."
4. STOP and wait for the owner's approval.
5. Once approved, append the rule to memory/lessons.md with the date.
6. Re-check the open leads in memory/leads-log.md, and list any whose next message should change because of the new rule.
