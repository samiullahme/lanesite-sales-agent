# Add-ons and gentle upsell

## Add-ons (1,500 rupees each, {billing: one-time / per month})
| Add-on | What they get | Pain it fixes |
|---|---|---|
| SEO setup | Google Business Profile setup/optimisation, site titles and descriptions, a local keyword for each page, Google Maps listing | "Google pe naam nahi aata", "naye customer kahan se layen" |
| WhatsApp auto-reply | Greeting message, away message, quick replies for price/menu/timing/location, link to the website | "raat ko message aate hain, reply nahi ho pata", "same sawal baar baar" |
| Social media management | {posts_per_month} posts a month, captions, website link in bio and posts | "posting ka time nahi milta", "page purana lagta hai" |

Never promise rankings, followers or sales from any add-on. Promise only the work we will do.
In voice notes, say the price as "pandrah sau rupay" / "پندرہ سو روپے".

## When to upsell (gentle rules)
1. **Only after the main website is a "yes"**: after they agree to go live or have paid. Never in the first message, never during an objection, never before they've seen the demo.
2. **One add-on at a time**, chosen to match a pain the customer *actually mentioned* in this chat. If they mentioned no pain that fits, don't upsell now; you can mention it once in a later follow-up instead.
3. **Make it an offer, not a push**: one sentence on the pain, one on the add-on, then a soft question.
4. **Treat "no" as final**: "Bilkul, koi baat nahi" and move on. Don't offer the same add-on again in this conversation, and don't offer a different add-on in the same message.
5. **At most 2 add-on offers per customer in total.** Log them in memory/leads-log.md (`upsell: seo-offered/declined/accepted`).
6. If they're a price-hunter, or asked for a discount, skip upselling until the website is live and they're happy.
7. Add-on discounts or bundles are only through the owner (escalate).

## Matching pain to add-on
- Asked about Google / new customers → SEO setup
- Complained about too many messages / late replies → WhatsApp auto-reply
- Said they have no time to post / page is inactive → Social media management

## Examples (Roman Urdu; mirror their script)
**After a yes, when they had mentioned Google:**
"Shukriya, In sha Allah kal tak live ho jayegi. Aap ne Google ka zikr kiya tha. Agar chahein to hum aapki Google Maps listing aur SEO bhi set kar dete hain, pandrah sau rupay mein. Saath karwa lein ya pehle website dekh lein?"

**When they had mentioned messages:**
"Ek cheez aur, aap ne kaha tha raat ko messages ka reply nahi ho pata. WhatsApp pe auto-reply laga dete hain jo price aur website ka link khud bhej de. Pandrah sau ka hai. Laga doon?"

**Follow-up a week after going live (only if no add-on has been offered yet):**
"Assalam o Alaikum! Website kaisi chal rahi hai? Agar posting ka time na milta ho to hum page bhi sambhal sakte hain, pandrah sau mein. Bas bata dijiyega."

**After a no:** "Bilkul, koi baat nahi. Website mein kuch chahiye ho to bataiye."
