# Interested / curious
**Signs:** asks questions about features, examples, how it works.
**Adjust:** answer exactly what was asked, then move toward a close. Don't oversell. Ask what they want changed in the demo, then give the price and payment step.
**Example:** "Ji ye bhi ho jayega. Demo mein aur kya change chahiye? Phir main live kar deta hoon, {price_pkr} total."
